Privacy Policy for Aura Health

Last Updated: May 2026

At Aura Health ("we", "our", or "us"), your privacy and the security of your health data are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Aura Health mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address, nickname, and profile photo used for authentication and account management.
  • Health & Vitals Data: Blood pressure readings, heart rate, weight logs, height, date of birth, and personal health notes entered into the app.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics regarding how you interact with the application to help us improve the user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the Aura Health experience. We use your health data to generate personal analytics, calculate BMI trends, and create exportable health reports (PDFs) at your request.

3. Data Security and Storage

Your health data is securely stored using industry-standard encryption protocols (via Firebase/Google Cloud). We do not store your data on public servers, and access is strictly limited to your authenticated account.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal or health data. We do not share your health data with advertisers or third-party marketers. Data is only shared with third-party service providers (like database hosting) necessary to operate the app, who are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Rights and Controls

You have full control over your data. Within the Aura Health app, you can edit, delete, or export your health logs at any time. You may also request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data via the app settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at team.aurahealth@gmail.com.
